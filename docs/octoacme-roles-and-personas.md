# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers validate that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for functional and non-functional coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Partner with Developers on shift-left testing and test automation opportunities
- Support release readiness with smoke/regression validation

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production escapes
- Maintain confidence in release quality
- Improve testability and feedback speed

### Typical Communication
- Daily collaboration with Developers during implementation
- Test status updates in standups and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage with PM, PdM, and engineering partners

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team process, remove delivery blockers, and coach teams on effective agile practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Track blockers and coordinate removal with the right owners
- Help the team maintain predictable iteration flow
- Drive continuous improvement actions from retrospectives

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on cadence, risk visibility, and follow-through
- Works with Product Managers to keep backlog refinement healthy
- Supports Developers and QA by escalating impediments quickly

### Typical Communication
- Daily blocker follow-ups
- Sprint health check-ins with PM and PdM
- Retrospective action tracking shared with the team

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning and execution so deployments are predictable, low-risk, and well-communicated.

### Responsibilities
- Define release scope, sequencing, and go/no-go readiness checkpoints
- Coordinate release windows, dependencies, and rollback plans
- Confirm deployment and validation owners before cutover
- Publish release notes and status updates to stakeholders

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Aligns with Project Managers on timelines, risks, and dependency readiness
- Works with Developers, QA, and DevOps on release execution details
- Coordinates with Product Managers and Stakeholders on release expectations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and checklists
- Go/no-go updates during release windows
- Post-release summaries with outcomes and follow-up actions

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain delivery infrastructure and operational practices that enable reliable, observable, and repeatable releases.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Improve observability, alerting, and operational runbooks
- Support environment consistency and incident response readiness
- Partner on reliability improvements and operational risk reduction

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on build/deploy reliability and tooling
- Supports Release Managers during release execution and rollback planning
- Partners with QA on stable test environments and release gates

### Typical Communication
- Pipeline health and incident updates in delivery channels
- Operational readiness checkpoints ahead of releases
- Reliability trend reviews with PM and engineering leads

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure solutions are usable and aligned with customer needs through research, flows, and interface guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Create and iterate user flows, wireframes, and interaction concepts
- Validate designs through user feedback and usability signals
- Define UX acceptance considerations with Product Managers
- Collaborate during implementation to preserve intended experience

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on problem framing and prioritization
- Works with Developers on implementation trade-offs and UX feasibility
- Coordinates with QA on usability-focused acceptance checks

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and walkthroughs with cross-functional teams
- Shared UX artifacts linked to backlog items
- Feedback summaries from user research or testing

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Champions embed practical security practices into day-to-day delivery and help teams prevent avoidable security risk.

### Responsibilities
- Raise security considerations during planning and design reviews
- Promote secure coding and dependency hygiene practices
- Coordinate threat modeling and security testing touchpoints
- Support incident response triage with security stakeholders

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and QA on security requirements and test coverage
- Works with Project Managers to surface security risks and mitigation plans
- Aligns with Release Managers and DevOps on secure release controls

### Typical Communication
- Security risk updates in planning and release checkpoints
- Escalation of critical findings to PM, PdM, and leadership as needed
- Follow-up tracking for remediation commitments

---

## Stakeholder (Business)

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders provide domain context, priorities, and approvals to keep project outcomes aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business objectives, constraints, and success criteria
- Provide timely decisions on scope, trade-offs, and priority changes
- Review milestone progress and confirm delivered value
- Escalate cross-team dependencies when leadership support is needed

### Interactions With Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate outcomes and priorities
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timeline, risks, and decision deadlines
- Participates in demos and release reviews with delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and monthly business reviews
- Decision and approval updates at key checkpoints
- Escalation conversations when risk or scope materially changes
